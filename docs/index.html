N E S W

Four fronts. One grid.

This is not a single-lane drip. Waves choose a cardinal direction, then pathfind around the towers you drop. Block a corridor and they find another.

  • Pathfinding pressure A* routing recalculates when you place or merge towers—your layout is the puzzle.
  • Merge & escalate Stack matching towers to climb tiers and change how you hold a lane.
  • Built for fingers Touch-first mobile build via Kivy, plus a full desktop pygame prototype.
